# Velora Botanicals — Store Policies (sample data)

Velora Botanicals is a fictional Indian skincare and haircare D2C brand used for this demo. Swap in your own brand and policies if you like.

**Demo "today" date:** 2 November 2026 (use this for return windows and delivery dates).

## Shipping
- Free shipping on orders of Rs 499 and above. Below that, Rs 59.
- Orders are packed within 24 hours (Mon–Sat).
- Delivery time: Metro cities 2–4 days, Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities 4–6 days, Tier 3 and rural 6–8 days.
- Couriers: Delhivery, Blue Dart, Ecom Express, India Post.
- India only. No international shipping.

## Cash on delivery (COD)
- Available across India on orders up to Rs 3,000.
- COD fee: Rs 49 per order.
- Every COD order gets a short confirmation call (by the Ringg voice agent) before dispatch to confirm the order, address and a good delivery time.
- If the customer cannot be reached after 2 call attempts in 24 hours, the order is held and a WhatsApp message is sent.
- Paying online saves the COD fee and gives an extra 5% off with code PREPAID5.

## Failed delivery (NDR)
- If a delivery attempt fails, the customer gets a call within 2 hours to confirm address, landmark and preferred time.
- The courier makes up to 2 more attempts. After that the order returns to the warehouse (RTO) and COD orders are cancelled.

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled until they are **packed**. Status "Placed" = can cancel. "Packed", "Shipped" or later = cannot cancel.
- Prepaid refunds for cancellations: 5–7 working days to the original payment method.

## Returns
- Return window: **7 days from delivery**.
- Product must be unused with the seal intact, **except** for:
  - damaged, leaking or wrong item received (photo needed, report within 48 hours of delivery), or
  - a skin reaction (photo needed). These are always handed to a human agent.
- Wrong shade of tinted products: free exchange within 7 days if unused.
- Kits can only be returned as a full kit.
- Return pickup is scheduled within 2–3 working days.

## Refunds
- Prepaid orders: refund to original payment method within 5–7 working days after the return reaches the warehouse.
- COD orders: refund to UPI or bank account shared by the customer, within 5–7 working days.
- Refunds above Rs 1,500 need approval from a human agent.

## Skin safety rules (the assistant must follow)
- Never diagnose skin conditions or promise medical results.
- Always suggest a 24-hour patch test before serums and treatments.
- Pregnant or breastfeeding: avoid Retinol Night Serum and Salicylic Acid Spot Gel; advise checking with a doctor.
- Any burning, rash, swelling or allergy: tell the customer to stop using and rinse, see a doctor if severe, and hand over to a human immediately.
- Under 16: suggest a dermatologist before using actives.

## Support hours (human team)
- Monday to Saturday, 10 am – 7 pm IST. Outside these hours, the assistant creates a ticket and promises a reply by the next working day by 12 noon.
